## Q1. SENA expansion (2002–2010) and later changes

### Takeaway
SENA clearly grew under Uribe (2002–2010). Instructor contracts tripled and training hours more than doubled by 2005. By 2013–2015 virtual training reached about 3 million slots a year. However, I found no verified year-by-year national series of *cupos* or *aprendices*, and no causal study of the expansion itself. The best causal SENA studies use selection cutoffs or the Jóvenes en Acción (JeA) eligibility cutoffs (see Q5).

### Cited Findings
- [F] SENA training hours delivered rose from about 5.7 million (2002) to about 12.7 million (2005). Contracted instructors rose from 6,166 (2003) to 18,688 (2010). The 2010 budget line for "ampliación de cobertura" was COP 34.5 billion. These figures come from a SENA presidency report presentation (Feb 2016) hosted by the SINDESENA union. — [SENA Informe de Presidencia Feb 2016 (search-only)](https://sindesena.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/11.-PRESENTACION-INFORME-DE-PRESIDENCIA-FEBRERO-DE-2016.pdf)
- [F] Virtual training slots: 3,121,374 (2013), 3,760,520 (2014), 3,101,887 (2015). Certification rates were 25%, 22% and 17%. — [same report (search-only)](https://sindesena.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/11.-PRESENTACION-INFORME-DE-PRESIDENCIA-FEBRERO-DE-2016.pdf)
- [F] SENA's role grew after 2002 under Uribe and was tied to higher-education coverage targets. A 2021 UPN master's thesis covers SENA and the 2002–2010 tertiary coverage strategy, with enrollment tables for T&T/HEIs/SENA for 2003–2011. — [UPN thesis (search-only)](https://repositorio.upn.edu.co/bitstreams/8e5ca800-a644-4142-be30-12334d3879dd/download); [UPN journal article (search-only)](https://revistas.upn.edu.co/index.php/RCE/article/download/4969/4055/13538)
- [F] National technical and technological (T&T) enrollment, all providers, rose from about 542,600 (2010) to 650,400 (2020) (MEN document, 2023). — [MEN (search-only)](https://www.mineducacion.gov.co/1780/articles-415961_recurs_16.pdf)
- [F] Saavedra & Medina (2012), "Formación para el Trabajo en Colombia" (Banco de la República Borradores de Economía 740; also CEDE). It describes the public supply of training (SENA titulada and complementaria) and its target population of vulnerable youth aged about 16–25. — [IDEAS: Borrador 740 (search-only)](https://ideas.repec.org/p/bdr/borrec/740.html); [PDF (search-only)](https://d1b4gd4m8561gs.cloudfront.net/sites/default/files/publicaciones/archivos/be_740.pdf)
- [F] Other SENA returns studies: "Evaluating the impact of SENA on earnings and Employment" (Universidad de los Andes/CEDE RePEc series; authors not seen in the snippet). An Icesi thesis (2016) using IV found that SENA courses are significantly associated with wages. A Uninorte (IEEC) study using the MEN 2014 graduate survey found that SENA graduates spend less time unemployed than comparable HEI graduates. — [IDEAS (search-only)](https://ideas.repec.org/p/col/000118/002646.html); [Icesi (search-only)](https://repository.icesi.edu.co/bitstreams/5f9b8cf2-8e36-7785-e053-2cc003c84dc5/download); [Uninorte (search-only)](https://manglar.uninorte.edu.co/bitstream/handle/10584/10243/1140846912.pdf?sequence=1)
- [F] SENA data reports produced with BIBB (Germany) exist for 2019–2023 and may hold recent enrollment series. — [BIBB/SENA 2023 (search-only)](https://www.bibb.de/dokumente/pdf/SENA_reporte_de_datos_bibb_2023.pdf); [2022 (search-only)](https://www.bibb.de/dokumente/pdf/SENA_reporte_de_datos_bibb_2022.pdf)

### Inferences
- [I] **Exploitable variation:** SENA capacity grew unevenly across its regional centres and municipalities: instructors went from 6k to 19k, and there were new centres and new tecnólogo offers. A shift-share or "distance-to-SENA-centre × cohort" design is conceivable. You would need centre-level supply data from SENA (Sofia Plus, Informes de gestión). Exposure would be the cohort turning 16–18 in a municipality when local slots expanded. Virtual courses (2008+) are national and hard to use for identification.
- [I] **Promise for NINI study: MEDIUM.** The policy is large and lands exactly at ages 16–25. But the rollout was not rule-based, so identification depends on building a supply instrument, which risks endogeneity to local demand. Mass virtual complementaria courses (certification rates around 20%) probably changed "studying" status only marginally.

### Gaps
- No verified annual series of SENA titulada/complementaria cupos for 2002–2010. A commonly repeated "1.1M → 7.9M cupos" type of claim was **not found**; do not use it without SENA Informes de gestión.
- No causal study of the 2002–2010 expansion on youth employment or NEET status was found.
- Dates for the creation of SENA tecnólogo programs and of the virtual platform were not verified.

## Q2. Articulación de la media con el SENA / doble titulación; media técnica; Fortalecimiento de la Educación Media; Bogotá "Media Fortalecida"

### Takeaway
Articulation of grades 10–11 with SENA (doble titulación) is a long-running, school-by-school program with local roots around 2001–2005. MEN describes a "Programa Nacional de Articulación del SENA con la Educación Media Técnica" by 2006. It has no verified national coverage series and **no rigorous causal evaluation found**. A DNP-commissioned evaluation compares participants with non-participants. Bogotá's Media Fortalecida (Proyecto 891, 2012–2016) rolled out by school, with 80k–85k students reported. Its staggered, school-level adoption makes it one of the most promising designs, but I found no impact evaluation of it either.

### Cited Findings
- [F] SENA describes "Articulación con la Media – Doble Titulación": students graduate with both a bachiller diploma and a SENA técnico certificate. — [SENA page (search-only)](https://www.sena.edu.co/es-co/formacion/Paginas/articulacionMedia.aspx)
- [F] Legal hooks cited by schools and municipalities include Ley 115 de 1994, art. 32 (media técnica), and Ley 812 de 2003 (PND), art. 8 lit. b num. 9 (articulation of SENA with media técnica). One Caldas school reports SENA-articulated cohorts enrolled in 2001 and again in 2003. — [Caldas school PA Media Técnica (search-only)](https://media.master2000.net/menu/268/2933/mper_132814_P.A.%20MEDIA%20T%C3%89CNICA.pdf); [MEN 2006 doc on "Programa Nacional de Articulación del SENA con la Educación Media Técnica" (search-only)](https://www.mineducacion.gov.co/1621/articles-106760_archivo_pdf4.pdf)
- [F] A Neiva study refers to "convenio 002 de 2005" between SENA and the Secretaría de Educación, which shows local agreements by ETC around 2005. — [Dialnet (search-only)](https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/8686778.pdf)
- [F] The potential articulation pool was about 950,000 youth entering media in 2010 (MEN data cited by a Bogotá Chamber of Commerce document). This is a pool, not enrollment. — [core.ac.uk (search-only)](https://core.ac.uk/download/158189068.pdf)
- [F] Barranquilla: about 10,000 graduates with doble titulación, with plans for +2,100 cupos in 2020 and +2,100 in 2021 (about 13,500 total). — [El Heraldo 2019 (search-only)](https://www.elheraldo.co/barranquilla/2019/09/30/sena-y-distrito-abren-2-100-cupos-mas-para-estudiantes-668978/)
- [F] Departmental regulation exists (e.g., Córdoba, Circular 000269 de 2018 on articulation with SENA). There is also a MEN 2021 presentation on doble titulación with departmental tables and a 2020 certification action plan; its text was too garbled to read the national totals. — [Córdoba circular (search-only)](https://seducacion.cordoba.gov.co/_contenido/noticias/2018/Circular_000269_2018_articulacion_SENA.pdf); [MEN Escuela de Secretarías 2021 (search-only)](https://escuelasecretarias.mineducacion.gov.co/sites/default/files/2022-10/4mar21%20Doble%20titulaci%C3%B3n%20.pdf); [MEN 2022 Doble Titulación technical note (search-only)](https://ote.mineducacion.gov.co/sites/default/files/otepublic/2022-06/Doble-Titulacion.pdf)
- [F] **DNP evaluation**: "Evaluación de la Estrategia de Articulación de la Educación Media con la Educación Superior / Técnica". Per the snippets it compares participants and non-participants, and differences often favour graduates of the program. I could not see the method, year or authors (fetch blocked). — [DNP (search-only, fetch blocked)](https://observatoriodefamilia.dnp.gov.co/Documents/Evaluaciones/Empleo/InFinal_Articulaci%C3%B3n%20educaci%C3%B3n%20Media%20%E2%80%93%20T%C3%A9cnica.pdf)
- [F] An ILO mid-term evaluation of the SENATEC project surveyed 941 rights holders, 644 of them articulation students. — [ILO (search-only)](https://webapps.ilo.org/ievaldiscovery/files/file/2383602)
- [F] The local and qualitative literature (Quindío, Planadas-Tolima, Villavicencio, Armenia, Bogotá) reports weak follow-up of graduates, low teacher awareness, and weak process design. A Ruta Maestra article questions whether placement figures can be attributed to the program. — [UNAD Quindío (search-only)](https://repository.unad.edu.co/bitstream/10596/85311/1/amgomezgi.pdf); [UNAD Armenia (search-only)](https://repository.unad.edu.co/jspui/bitstream/10596/53788/1/vhmartinezp.pdf); [Ruta Maestra (search-only)](https://rutamaestra.santillana.com.co/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Rupturas-y-retos-de-la-articulaci%C3%B3n-en-la-educaci%C3%B3n-media-en-colombia.pdf)
- [F] **Fortalecimiento a la Educación Media (MEN):** MEN's program provides technical assistance to secretariats and schools. The Fondo FEM funds a "basic" and a "technical" component; in 2022 funding was limited to the basic component plus technical assistance (nivelación courses). — [MEN Fortalecimiento a la educación media (search-only)](https://www.mineducacion.gov.co/1759/w3-article-374737.html)
- [F] **Bogotá Media Fortalecida (Proyecto 891, Bogotá Humana, 2012–2016):** it aimed to make media in district schools "diversa, electiva y homologable" with higher education. It had three components: Media Diversa (grades 10–11), an optional grade 12, and access/permanence in higher education. The target was 80,000 grade 10–11 students plus access for 30,000 strata 1–3 students. A 2015 bulletin reports 85,000 beneficiaries and 7,165 graduates using the optional grade 12 in 21 district schools. These are administration-reported figures. — [SED Bogotá doc (search-only)](https://www.mineducacion.gov.co/1780/articles-302596_archivo_pdf_bogota_SED.pdf); [Bogotá.gov.co 2015 (search-only)](https://bogota.gov.co/historico-alcaldia/45-mil-estudiantes-de-ultimo-grado-fortalecen-conocimientos-en-compete)
- [F] OECD *Education at a Glance 2022*: Colombia's average graduation age from vocational upper secondary is 16, against an OECD average of 22, and all vocational upper secondary graduates have direct access to tertiary. — [OECD EAG 2022 (search-only)](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/education-at-a-glance-2022_3197152b-en/full-report/component-50.html)
- [F] OECD *Education in Colombia* (2016) recommends building local capacity to connect schools with tertiary institutions and employers to ease transitions. — [Scribd copy of highlights (search-only)](https://es.scribd.com/document/577871856/Education-in-Colombia-Highlights-Es)

### Inferences
- [I] **Exploitable variation (articulation):** adoption varies by **school × year × program (specialty)** and by ETC agreements (e.g., convenio 002/2005 in Neiva). With SIMAT (school-level enrollment, specialty flag) linked to Saber 11, SPADIES/SNIES and PILA (the MEN "Observatorio Laboral" link), a staggered DiD or event study at the school level is feasible. Outcomes would be the cohort's post-graduation tertiary enrollment and formal employment at ages 17–20, i.e. exactly where the NINI rate jumps. Selection of schools into articulation is a threat, so use within-school pre-trends.
- [I] **Bogotá Media Fortalecida** offers a clean setting: a well-defined start (2012–2013), a list of participating colegios distritales that rolled in over time, a grade-12 option at 21 schools, and Bogotá's rich admin data. A staggered DiD across district schools on NEET at 17–19 (using SED + PILA + SNIES) is plausible.
- [I] Because Colombia graduates students from media around age 16–17 (OECD EAG), the NINI spike between 16 and 18 coincides with **leaving media**. Policies operating in grades 10–11 (articulation) or extending media (optional grade 12) are therefore directly on the margin.
- [I] **Promise: HIGH** for articulation and Media Fortalecida, conditional on obtaining school-level adoption dates. This is policy-relevant and well timed, and there is a gap: no causal evaluation was found.

### Gaps
- No verified national start date or legal instrument for the national SENA–media articulation program. Candidates are Ley 812 de 2003 (PND) and the program described in MEN's 2006 document; this needs checking.
- No verified national coverage series (students, schools, ETCs by year).
- The DNP evaluation's method, authors and year are unknown (fetch blocked).
- No impact evaluation of Bogotá Media Fortalecida was found (only case studies).
- The program was renamed later (e.g., "doble titulación" under Duque; changes under Petro). This was not verified.

## Q3. Ley 749 de 2002 (ciclos propedéuticos), Ley 1064 de 2006 (ETDH), Decreto 4904 de 2009

### Takeaway
These laws restructured supply-side regulation: propedeutic cycles in T&T higher education, recognition of non-formal "Educación para el Trabajo y el Desarrollo Humano" (ETDH), and quality and registration rules for ETDH. They are national, so there is no within-country rollout except through program registration dates.

### Cited Findings
- [F] **Ley 749 de 2002** organizes técnica profesional and tecnológica higher education. It introduced propedeutic cycles in engineering, IT and administration, which Ley 1188 de 2008 extended to all areas. Parts were struck down (C-852-05, effects deferred to 16 Dec 2006; C-782-07 on an expression in art. 8). — [Función Pública: Ley 749 (search-only)](https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=6833); [MEN ciclos propedéuticos (search-only)](https://www.mineducacion.gov.co/1759/w3-article-196476.html)
- [F] **Ley 1064 de 2006** supports and strengthens ETDH, which the Ley General de Educación had called "educación no formal". — [Colombia Aprende ETDH (search-only)](https://www.colombiaaprende.edu.co/sites/default/files/files_public/archivos/etdh/web/92408.html)
- [F] **Decreto 4904 de 2009** regulates the organization, supply and functioning of ETDH (program registration requirements). It is now compiled in Decreto 1075 de 2015, Libro 2, Parte 6. — [La Salle document citing it (search-only)](https://cmspreprod.lasalle.edu.co/sites/default/files/2024-08/5-ACS-009_2024_0.pdf)
- [F] Some sources refer to articulation via "Decreto 749 de 2002". This is probably a mislabel of **Ley** 749 de 2002 [NV]. — [core.ac.uk (search-only)](https://core.ac.uk/download/158189068.pdf)

### Inferences
- [I] **Variation:** registration dates of propedeutic or ETDH programs by institution and municipality (SNIES / SIET registries) could give a local-supply measure. This is weaker than articulation.
- [I] **Promise: LOW–MEDIUM.** These are national, gradual supply rules with no age or eligibility threshold. They may be most useful as controls or as context for the T&T expansion.

### Gaps
- No evaluation linking these laws to youth enrollment, employment or NEET status was found.
- ETDH enrollment series (SIET) were not located.

## Q4. Contrato de aprendizaje / cuota de aprendices: Ley 789 de 2002 → Ley 2466 de 2025 → Decreto 223 de 2026

### Takeaway
Ley 789 de 2002 created the modern apprenticeship quota, with a **firm-size threshold of 15 workers**. A published RD paper (Ospino) exploits that threshold for firm outcomes. The 2025 labor reform (Ley 2466) made the apprenticeship a special fixed-term **labor contract**, raised the stipend to 75% of the minimum wage (lectiva) and 100% (productiva), and changed monetization rules. Decreto 223 de 2026 regulated it. The reported early effect is fewer apprenticeship contracts and more monetization. The size of the fall is disputed: about −3% (SENA) versus −15% (congresswoman Juvinao).

### Cited Findings
- [F] Ley 789 de 2002, art. 32, sets which firms are obligated: private employers with **no fewer than 15 workers**. Art. 33 sets the quota, determined by SENA. A paragraph allows firms with fewer than 10 workers to take an apprentice voluntarily. — [Consejo de Estado ruling summary (search-only)](https://www.consejodeestado.gov.co/documentos/boletines/PDF/11001-03-25-000-2003-00274-01(2799-03).pdf); [Función Pública, Decreto 1334 de 2018 on quota determination (search-only)](https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=87680)
- [F/NV] The quota is reported as **1 apprentice per 20 workers**, with firms under 15 workers voluntary (Mondaq summary). I did not find the official text of art. 33; the exact formula, including the fraction ≥10 rule, is **not verified**. — [Mondaq (search-only)](https://www.mondaq.com/unitedstates/employee-benefits-compensation/1653760/colombias-labor-reform-sena-apprentices-what-employers-need-to-know)
- [F] Before 2025 the stipend ("apoyo de sostenimiento") in the **practical phase** depended on the national unemployment rate (art. 30, Ley 789). It was 75% of the minimum wage (SMMLV), or 100% if unemployment the previous year was below 10%. Example: from 1 Jan 2020 firms could pay only 75% (Garrigues). For 2024 it stayed at 75% because certified 2023 unemployment was 10.2%. — [Garrigues 2020 (search-only)](https://www.garrigues.com/es_ES/noticia/colombia-disminuye-el-apoyo-de-sostenimiento-para-aprendices-del-sena-en-la-fase-practica-en); [Actualícese 2024 (search-only)](https://actualicese.com/apoyo-de-sostenimiento-para-aprendices-2024/)
- [F] **Ospino (2018)**, "The Effects of Being Subject to the Colombian Apprenticeship Contract on Manufacturing Firm Performance", CEDLAS WP 230. RD/ITT exploiting the 2002 reform and the regulation design. It finds output per worker +10 log points, TFP +3 log points, export share +2pp, and the directly hired wage bill −9 log points. A companion paper links the reform to more outsourced labour contracts. A separate Fedesarrollo study (Gaviria, 2005) and a CIDSE (Cali) DiD study (2007) assess Ley 789. — [CEDLAS WP 230 (search-only)](https://www.cedlas.econo.unlp.edu.ar/wp/wp-content/uploads/doc_cedlas230.pdf); [IDEAS listing of related article (search-only)](https://ideas.repec.org/a/col/000438/013402.html)
- [F] **Ley 2466 de 2025** (labor reform) has been in force since **25 June 2025** (one source). Littler says contracts signed before 26 June 2025 follow the old rules [minor date conflict]. Art. 21 amended CST art. 81: the apprenticeship becomes a **special, fixed-term labor contract**, at most 3 years, with social security. Art. 23 amended Ley 789 art. 34: a firm that does not hire must pay SENA **1.5 SMMLV per missing apprentice per month** (monetization). One source says 25% of monetization revenue goes to SENA apprentice support. MinTrabajo issued Circular 0083 of 18 July 2025 as implementing guidance. — [ICEF, sanction 26 Jun 2025 (search-only)](https://icef.com.co/component/k2/item/7904-ley-2466-gobierno-sanciona-la-reforma-laboral); [Asuntos Legales (search-only)](https://www.asuntoslegales.com.co/actualidad/asi-cambian-los-contratos-de-aprendizaje-con-la-laboral-4197041); [Circular 0083/2025 (search-only)](https://accounter.co/descarga/42743/circulares/131903/circular-no-0083-de-2025_contrato-de-aprendizaje-2-1-1-1); [Función Pública: Ley 2466 (search-only)](https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma_pdf.php?i=269376)
- [F] Stipend under Ley 2466: **75% SMMLV (lectiva) and 100% SMMLV (productiva)**, up from 50% and 75%. With the 2026 minimum wage of COP 1,750,905, that is about COP 1,313,179 and COP 1,750,905. El Cronista instead describes "75% first year / 100% second year" [NV conflict]. — [El Tiempo (search-only)](https://www.eltiempo.com/economia/finanzas-personales/cuanto-gana-un-aprendiz-del-sena-si-el-sueldo-corresponde-al-75-del-salario-minimo-3565506); [Infobae 7 Jan 2026 (search-only)](https://www.infobae.com/colombia/2026/01/07/esto-es-lo-que-ganara-un-aprendiz-del-sena-en-2026-segun-la-reforma-laboral-y-el-ajuste-de-salario-minimo/)
- [F] **Decreto 223 de 2026** (MinTrabajo, reported dated 5 March 2026) comprehensively regulates internships and the apprenticeship contract, including **dual training**, which Ley 2466 art. 21 defines as training jointly planned and run by the private sector and SENA or an educational institution. It sets at least 75% SMMLV in the first year of dual training and requires ARL coverage throughout. — [Función Pública: Decreto 223/2026 (search-only)](https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=272856); [Accounter editorial (search-only)](https://accounter.co/noticias/editorial/decreto-223-de-2026-nueva-regulacion-integral-de-practicas-laborales-y-contrato-de-aprendizaje-en-colombia)
- [F] **Early descriptive evidence:** SENA reports 392,056 apprentices in 2024 versus 379,800 in 2025 (−3.13%). Rep. Cathy Juvinao estimates −15.2% and +68% monetization; SENA says her 342,384 figure was a preliminary report. HK Law, citing Semana, reports more than 8,000 firms choosing to pay instead of hire. A union document says about 260,000 contracts were registered in 2026, around 70% of the annual target. — [El País Cali (search-only)](https://www.elpais.com.co/colombia/contratacion-de-aprendices-del-sena-se-desploma-un-152-cathy-juvinao-denuncia-que-las-cifras-oficiales-eran-falsas-2830.html); [El Colombiano (search-only)](https://www.elcolombiano.com/negocios/reforma-laboral-aprendices-sena-caida-contratos-monetizacion-colombia-PL33832519); [Valora Analitik (search-only)](https://www.valoraanalitik.com/?p=556908); [HK Law, Jun 2026 (search-only)](https://www.hklaw.com/es/news/intheheadlines/2026/06/la-reforma-laboral-hundio-el-contrato-de-aprendizaje)

### Inferences
- [I] **Exploitable variation:**
  - (a) **Firm-size RD at 15 workers** (and quota steps by workforce size), on the demand side.
  - (b) **Time variation in the practical-phase stipend** (75% vs 100% SMMLV depending on whether the previous year's unemployment was below 10%), 2003–2024. It is national but rule-based and switches on and off with the unemployment threshold.
  - (c) **The 2025 reform** raises the cost per apprentice. It can be tested as an event study with dose = municipality/sector pre-reform reliance on apprentices (share of PILA apprentice contributions among youth 16–24), on youth employment and NEET in 2025–2026 (GEIH).
  - (d) The **contract-start rule** (before vs after 25/26 June 2025) is a sharp date cutoff, useful for apprentice-level analysis in PILA.
- [I] The apprenticeship is the main formal "study + work" status for 16–24-year-olds. Apprentices are counted as studying and/or working, so a contraction directly reclassifies some youth toward NINI if they are not absorbed elsewhere.
- [I] **Promise: HIGH for the 2025 reform** (topical, sharp date, admin data via PILA/SENA; but only a short post-period and many simultaneous reform components). **MEDIUM for the 15-worker RD** (studied for firms, not yet for youth NEET; needs employer-employee data).

### Gaps
- The official text of Ley 789 art. 33 (exact quota formula) was not opened.
- The exact sanction date of Ley 2466 (24/25/26 June 2025) has conflicting snippets. Decreto 223 de 2026's date was seen only in secondary sources.
- No official consolidated 2025–2026 apprentice series. Juvinao's and SENA's figures disagree.
- No study of the apprenticeship quota on youth NEET status was found.

## Q5. Jóvenes en Acción (2001/2002–2005 training; 2012–2023 CCT for SENA/tertiary) and Renta Joven (2024–)

### Takeaway
The original JeA (urban youth aged 18–25 in SISBEN 1–2; 3 months classroom + 3 months on-the-job; 2005 cohort randomized) has the strongest causal evidence of any Colombian TVET policy. It shows short- and long-run gains in formal employment and, for trainees, in later higher-education enrollment. The 2012+ version (DPS, Resolución 01970 de 2012) is a conditional transfer for poor youth enrolled in SENA or tertiary education. Its SISBEN-score eligibility has been exploited in an RD showing **+8 to 13pp SENA enrollment**. Renta Joven replaced it on 1 Jan 2024 (ages 14–28, bachiller, Sisbén).

### Cited Findings
- [F] **Original JeA:** a government-funded program with private providers delivering courses (3 months classroom + 3 months on-the-job). It trained youth aged 18–25 in SISBEN levels 1–2 between 2001 and 2005. J-PAL describes it as free vocational training for 18–25-year-olds. The program moved from the Presidency to SENA in 2006 and changed design so much that it was considered a different program. — [J-PAL evaluation page (search-only)](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/evaluation/vocational-training-disadvantaged-youth-colombia?lang=fr); [CEPAL program database (search-only)](https://dds.cepal.org/bpsnc/programme?id=68); [Ximena Peña, La Silla Vacía (search-only)](https://www.lasillavacia.com/?p=12971)
- [F] **Attanasio, Kugler & Meghir (2011)**, "Subsidizing Vocational Training for Disadvantaged Youth in Colombia: Evidence from a Randomized Trial", *AEJ: Applied* 3(3):188–220. RCT using 2005 course randomization. Women offered training earned about 18% more and men about 8% more (NBER WP 13931 version). Women saw higher formal employment. Effects work through on-the-job training. — [EconPapers AEJ (search-only)](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:aea:aejapp:v:3:y:2011:i:3:p:188-220); [IZA DP 4251 (search-only)](https://docs.iza.org/dp4251.pdf)
- [F] **Attanasio, Guarín, Medina & Meghir (2017)**, "Vocational Training for Disadvantaged Youth in Colombia: A Long-Term Follow-Up", *AEJ: Applied* 9(2):131–43. Large short-run effects persisted: more formal participation and social security contributions, and more work in larger firms. — [IDEAS (search-only)](https://ideas.repec.org/a/aea/aejapp/v9y2017i2p131-43.html)
- [F] **Kugler, Kugler, Saavedra & Herrera-Prada (2022)**, "Long-Term Educational Consequences of Vocational Training in Colombia: Impacts on Young Trainees and Their Relatives", *JHR* 57(1):178–216 (NBER WP 21607; earlier CEPR DP 10859, "Long-Term Direct and Spillover Effects of Job Training"). Randomization plus admin data: 11 years later, trainees had higher higher-education enrollment and relatives had higher secondary attainment. The IRR rises from 22.2% to 24.1% for women and from 10.2% to 25.5% for men when education gains are included. — [EconPapers JHR (search-only)](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:uwp:jhriss:v:57:y:2022:i:1:p:178-216); [NBER WP 21607 (search-only)](https://ideas.repec.org/p/nbr/nberwo/21607.html); [CEPR DP 10859 (search-only)](https://ideas.repec.org/p/cpr/ceprdp/10859.html)
- [F] **JeA 2012 redesign:** adopted by Resolución 01970 of 21 Nov 2012 (DPS), for bachilleres in poverty or vulnerability. Registration criteria were set by Resolución 00262 (2 Apr 2013) and enrollment timing by Resolución 01170 (29 Nov 2013). The DNP 2013 evaluation agenda included building the baseline for the JeA impact evaluation. A later operating manual was adopted by Resolución 00851 de 2022. — [Armenia: Res. 00851/2022 (search-only)](https://armenia.gov.co/wp-content/uploads/2023/2023_Desarrollo_social/RESOLUCION_No._00851_de_2022-adopta_manual_del_PROGRAMA_JOVENES_EN_ACCION.PDF); [DNP Agenda de Evaluaciones 2013 (search-only)](https://colaboracion.dnp.gov.co/CDT/Sinergia/Documentos/Agenda_de_Evaluaciones_2013_Inicial.pdf)
- [F/NV] The task brief mentions Ley 1532 de 2012. The snippet I found ties Ley 1532 de 2012 to Más Familias en Acción (the health and education of minors), **not** to JeA. Do not cite it as JeA's legal basis without checking. — [Rama Judicial tutela (search-only)](https://www.ramajudicial.gov.co/documents/10635/111279701/Fallo+tutela+Vs+DNP+22+050+PendienteCumplir+Fase+Subsidio+%281%29.pdf/31b67986-7866-45be-a97b-904050ec37c5)
- [F] **Gamboa & Millán-Quijano (2018; revised 2021 as "Liquidity constraints and free post-secondary education")**, UC3M working paper. RD using (i) the JeA eligibility cutoff (SISBEN score) and (ii) SENA's admission cutoff. JeA eligibility raises vocational (SENA) enrollment by **8–13pp** (11.9pp in the 2021 version). Only the JeA cutoff matters, which the authors read as liquidity constraints and opportunity costs. Labour effects are heterogeneous. — [UC3M e-archivo (search-only)](https://e-archivo.uc3m.es/handle/10016/26290); [IDEAS (search-only)](https://ideas.repec.org/p/cte/werepe/26290.html); [core.ac.uk PDF (search-only)](https://core.ac.uk/download/288500327.pdf)
- [F] University case studies (UIS 2012–2019, UFPS 2014–2018, Córdoba) report that JeA reduced dropout or improved permanence. These are local and mostly non-experimental. — [UIS thesis (search-only)](https://noesis.uis.edu.co/bitstreams/966494f2-04f7-49e6-a13d-6400aab6d9ca/download); [UFPS (search-only)](https://repositorio.ufps.edu.co/handle/ufps/1564)
- [F] **Renta Joven:** replaced JeA from 1 Jan 2024 within the Sistema de Transferencias. Reported requirements: age 14–28, bachiller completed, current Sisbén (or alternative targeting instrument). A 2024 registration window ran 20 Jul–15 Oct 2024, targeting 80k–100k new youth. Regulated by **Resolución 137 de 2024** [seen only in a summary, NV]. Sisbén IV uses groups A–D rather than scores. — [Bloomberg Línea (search-only)](https://www.bloomberglinea.com/latinoamerica/colombia/que-se-sabe-del-pago-de-renta-joven-en-enero-2024-antes-jovenes-en-accion/); [Portafolio (search-only)](https://www.portafolio.co/economia/finanzas/renta-joven-2024-requisitos-proceso-de-inscripcion-monto-y-otros-detalles-600333); [El Colombiano, "Gobierno reglamenta Renta Joven" (search-only)](https://www.elcolombiano.com/negocios/gobierno-reglamenta-renta-joven-que-es-y-quienes-pueden-recibirla-IG23704279)

### Inferences
- [I] **Exploitable variation:**
  - (a) **SISBEN III score cutoffs** (2012–~2021) and **Sisbén IV group thresholds** (2021+) give RD designs on SENA/tertiary enrollment and, if linked to PILA/GEIH-like data, on NEET status at 17–24.
  - (b) **Municipal rollout** of JeA 2012+ (it entered cities and municipalities in phases) gives a staggered DiD. The phase list was not verified.
  - (c) **The 2023→2024 transition to Renta Joven** (new rules, a registration freeze until July 2024) gives a cohort/date discontinuity.
  - (d) The original 2005 RCT already has long-run admin linkage, so NEET-type outcomes (no formal job and no enrollment) could in principle be built from the same data used by Kugler et al.
- [I] **Promise: HIGH.** There is a credible running variable (SISBEN), the target ages overlap the 16→18 NINI jump, and evidence already shows enrollment effects (Gamboa & Millán-Quijano). The natural extension is NEET as an outcome. JeA 2012+ requires being a bachiller, so it acts after the age-16–17 exit from media, exactly where NINI rises.

### Gaps
- Exact JeA 2012+ eligibility (age range, SISBEN III cutoffs by area, Red Unidos, victims, indigenous priority groups), transfer amounts and the municipal rollout schedule were not verified.
- The official DPS/DNP impact evaluation of JeA 2012+ (baseline 2013; possible follow-up around 2016–2017) was not located.
- Renta Joven's eligible Sisbén IV groups and amounts are not verified, and no evaluation was found.

## Q6. Sistema / Marco Nacional de Cualificaciones; dual-training pilots

### Takeaway
The National Qualifications System and Framework (SNC/MNC) was created in the 2018–2022 PND (Ley 1955 de 2019, art. 194). The framework was adopted by Decreto 1649 de 2021, and recognition of prior learning by Decreto 946 de 2022. Art. 194 was modified by Ley 2294 de 2023. Dual training gained a legal definition through Ley 2466 de 2025 and Decreto 223 de 2026. I found no evaluated dual-training pilot (e.g., AHK/German chamber).

### Cited Findings
- [F] Decreto 1649 de 2021 adopts and regulates the MNC under Ley 1955 de 2019 art. 194, adding Parte 7 to Libro 2 of Decreto 1075 de 2015. — [Función Pública: Decreto 1649/2021 (search-only)](https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=173957)
- [F] Decreto 946 de 2022 regulates recognition of prior learning (RAP) within the SNC. — [Función Pública: Decreto 946/2022 (search-only)](https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=187426)
- [F] Ley 2294 de 2023 (PND 2022–2026) modified art. 194 of Ley 1955 (per a MEN document). — [MEN (search-only)](https://www.mineducacion.gov.co/1780/articles-424601_recurso_01.pdf)
- [F] Ley 2466 de 2025 art. 21 defines dual training (see Q4). Decreto 223 de 2026 sets at least 75% SMMLV in year 1 of dual training. — [Accounter (search-only)](https://accounter.co/noticias/editorial/decreto-223-de-2026-nueva-regulacion-integral-de-practicas-laborales-y-contrato-de-aprendizaje-en-colombia)
- [F] A BIBB report (VET in Colombia, 2016) and a TUM thesis (Heindl 2018) describe Colombian VET, including SENA's "tecnoacademias" for students in the last two years of school. — [BIBB VET in Colombia 2016 (search-only)](https://bibb.de/dokumente/pdf/VET_in_Colombia_(as_of_2016).pdf); [TUM thesis (search-only)](https://www.edu.sot.tum.de/fileadmin/w00bed/td/riedl/Heindl2018VETinColombia.pdf)

### Inferences
- [I] **Promise: LOW.** The MNC is an institutional framework with no sharp eligibility rule, and its implementation was slow. Dual training is new (2025–2026) and too recent for outcomes. It may become relevant if adoption by sector or firm can be observed.

### Gaps
- No dual-training pilot (dates, sites, size) was verified, and no evaluation was found.
- No evidence was found of MNC implementation reaching learners at scale.

## Cross-cutting descriptive evidence and reviews

### Takeaway
International reviews exist (OECD 2016 *Education in Colombia*; OECD EAG 2022; BIBB/SENA data reports), but none I found measures NEET outcomes around specific TVET reforms. The descriptive gap is itself a contribution: link SIMAT/SENA/SNIES/PILA by cohort and age.

### Cited Findings
- [F] OECD *Education in Colombia* (2016) has chapters on upper secondary and tertiary education and recommends local school–tertiary–employer links. — [OECD highlights (search-only)](https://es.scribd.com/document/577871856/Education-in-Colombia-Highlights-Es)
- [F] OECD EAG 2022: vocational upper-secondary graduation age in Colombia is 16 (OECD 22). — [OECD (search-only)](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/education-at-a-glance-2022_3197152b-en/full-report/component-50.html)
- [F] An older secondary source (about 2013) says SENA covers about 60% of professional instruction through 118 centres. — [Oxford Business Group (search-only)](https://drupal.oxfordbusinessgroup.com/node/774685)

### Inferences
- [I] The age profile in Lasso-Valderrama & Vargas (2026), with NINI rising from 5% to 22% between 16 and 18, coincides with media graduation at about 16–17. Every program targeted at "bachilleres" (JeA 2012+, Renta Joven, SENA titulada, apprenticeships) acts *after* this exit. Articulation and Media Fortalecida act *before* it. A paper contrasting a pre-exit intervention (articulation, staggered by school) with a post-exit one (JeA SISBEN RD) would map where the NINI jump can be moved.

### Gaps
- No World Bank review specific to Colombian TVET was located in this pass.
- No paper measuring NEET/NINI as an outcome of any of these policies was found.
